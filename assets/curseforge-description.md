# Wowsenger

Chat with several friends at once, like a messenger. One Blizzard-style window holds your group chats and your whispers, with unread markers and history. There is no server: Wowsenger sends group messages straight to the other members' addons.

## What it does

- **Group chats.** Make a group with the friends you play with, also across guilds. Everyone in the group needs Wowsenger. Anyone in the group can add or remove people.
- **Offline friends catch up.** A friend who was offline gets the messages when they log in, from anyone in the group who is online at the same time.
- **Your whispers.** Character whispers and Battle.net whispers show up in the window too, one conversation per person, and still appear in your normal chat. These work with anyone, with or without Wowsenger.
- **Unread markers** on every conversation, and a small popup at the top of the screen when a message arrives while the window is closed. Drag the popup anywhere.
- **History** is kept between sessions and shared between your characters.

## Stays out of the way

- Nothing is sent during combat. It goes out right after.
- In boss fights and some instances the game can hold back addon messages; they arrive afterwards.
- Looks like the game's own windows.

## Getting started

1. Install Wowsenger on every friend's computer and restart the game.
2. Click the minimap button or type `/wowsenger`.
3. Click **+ New group**, give it a name and tick your friends.

## Commands

- `/wowsenger`: open or close the window
- `/wowsenger toast test`: show a sample popup (drag it where you want it)
- `/wowsenger toast reset`: put the popup back at the top
- `/wowsenger debug demo`: a demo group to try the window on your own (`/wowsenger debug demo off` removes it)
- `/wowsenger help`: all commands

## Good to know

- You only get group messages while at least one other member is online at the same time.
- At most 500 messages are kept per group.
