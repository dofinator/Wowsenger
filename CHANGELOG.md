# Wowsenger 0.2.0

The first version: group chats with your friends and your whispers, in one window.

## New

- **Group chats.** Click **+ New group**, give it a name and tick your friends (or type a name). Everyone in the group needs Wowsenger. Friends can be in different guilds.
- **Offline friends catch up.** A friend who was offline gets the group's messages when they log in, from anyone in the group who is online.
- **Your whispers too.** Character whispers and Battle.net whispers show up in the window as well, one conversation per person. They still appear in your normal chat.
- **Unread markers** on every conversation, and a small popup at the top of the screen when a message arrives while the window is closed. Drag the popup if you want it somewhere else.
- **History** is kept between sessions and shared between your characters.
- Right-click a conversation to edit or leave a group, invite to party or delete a conversation.
- Nothing is sent during combat; it goes out right after.

Open Wowsenger with the minimap button or `/wowsenger`. `/wowsenger debug demo` shows a demo group, so you can try the window on your own.
