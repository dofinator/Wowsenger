# Wowsenger 0.3.0

Easier to tidy up and easier to keep out of the way.

## New

- **Delete a conversation in one click.** Hover over a person in the list and click the cross. "Undo" stays at the bottom of the list for a few seconds, and it brings back everything you deleted in a row.
- **Minimize.** The new button next to X folds the window into a slim title bar that shows how many new messages you have. Click the bar (or the minimap button, or a popup) to unfold it. It stays folded after a reload.
- **Resize the window** by dragging its bottom-right corner. The size is remembered.
- **Type straight away.** Picking a conversation puts the cursor in the message box. Press Esc to leave it.

## Changed

- "Delete conversation" in the right-click menu no longer asks first; use Undo if you change your mind.
